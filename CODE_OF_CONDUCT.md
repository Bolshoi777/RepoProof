# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in RepoProof a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming and inclusive language, being
respectful of differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive
criticism, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy
towards other community members.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling,
insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or
private harassment, publishing others' private information without explicit
permission, and any conduct that could reasonably be considered
inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull
requests) and in public spaces when an individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported by opening a GitHub issue with
`[conduct]` in the title, describing the facts without private data of
third parties. Maintainers will review, may remove offending content, and
may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers who do not
follow or enforce this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
